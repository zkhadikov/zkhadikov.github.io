About

I design and evolve software architectures for Industrial Internet of Things products at Endress+Hauser Digital Solutions. My work combines technical direction with hands-on delivery across REST APIs, web applications, serverless AWS services, and customer-specific industrial solutions.

I care about more than code: mentoring engineers, clarifying architectural decisions, improving development practices, and helping teams deliver sustainable systems. Before focusing on IIoT, I led the development of Jedox Office integrations, established build and release automation, and worked on high-performance analytical database technology.

My background spans Ruby on Rails, JavaScript and TypeScript, Angular, React, Java, C#/.NET, C++, AWS, Docker, Jenkins, and Git-based delivery workflows. I hold degrees in Computer Science/Computational Science and Metrology and Quality Management, connecting software engineering with an understanding of industrial systems and processes.
